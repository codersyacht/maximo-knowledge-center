## Installation

- Download Firefix Thunderbird [here](https://download.mozilla.org/?product=thunderbird-151.0-SSL&os=osx&lang=en-US)
- Install Thunderbird by clicking Thunderbird.dmg. 
- Move Thunderbird to Applications.
- Launch Thunderbird.
